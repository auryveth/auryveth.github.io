Pilot criteria

A strong first workflow has four properties.

Workflow selection prioritizes measurable operational value, bounded risk and clear ownership.

Frequent

It happens often enough to produce learning and measurable evidence.

Bounded

The decisions and consequences have understandable limits.

Measurable

Time, errors, cycle time or outcome quality can be compared before and after.

Owned

A real business owner can define acceptable behavior and approve authority changes.

Illustrative pilot path

Progressive authority model

Stage 1

Map

Understand the workflow, systems, people, rules and failure modes.

Stage 2

Shadow

Generate recommendations without affecting production work.

Stage 3

Approval-gated

Prepare actions that a human approves before execution.

Stage 4

Bounded autonomy

Only qualified low-risk actions move into pre-approved execution.
