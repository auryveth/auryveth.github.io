Business Organisms

Autonomous operations, introduced one trusted capability at a time.

Auryveth's first commercial system is the business-organism specialization of its broader digital-organism research: a persistent operational system connected to approved business context, able to learn from outcomes over time, and governed by explicit authority boundaries.

In developmentSME-firstHuman-governed autonomy
Target architecture

One operational layer across fragmented business tools.

Email, ERP, CRM, spreadsheets, inventory, quotations, purchasing and follow-ups should become context for one persistent operating system rather than isolated automation scripts.

Shared operational context

Maintain a working model of approved customers, products, inventory, suppliers, workflows, exceptions and commitments.

Task continuity

Operational continuity is preserved across interactions and systems. The organism tracks open work, dependencies, approvals and outcomes over time.

Governed execution

Every class of action is constrained by permissions, thresholds, audit trails and explicit escalation paths.

General operating loop

From incoming work to governed execution.

Illustrative cross-industry workflow. The exact records, systems and actions change by organization; the governance pattern remains the same. This is a design objective, not a claim that every step is currently deployed.

1

Work arrives

Recognize the request, responsible context, deadline and permitted information sources.

2

Prepare work

Gather approved records, policies, availability, dependencies and constraints needed to proceed.

3

Request authority

Present the proposed action, relevant exceptions and supporting evidence to an authorized person.

4

Execute & learn

Carry out the permitted action, track the outcome, update operational state, and use feedback to improve future decisions.

Authority architecture

The organism does not grant itself permission.

Capability growth and authority growth are separate. A new skill can be evaluated in shadow mode before a manager chooses whether that skill receives execution authority.

Pilot scope

Validate one operating loop before expanding operational scope.

Initial pilots should focus on workflows with measurable volume, repeatable decisions, bounded risk and clear business ownership.

Suitable initial workflows

Quotations, order follow-up, stock checks, purchasing preparation, customer updates, recurring reporting and task coordination.

Human review by default

High-impact financial, contractual, pricing or customer commitments remain approval-gated until sufficient evidence exists.

Measure before expanding

Track time saved, cycle time, error rate, exceptions, human overrides and business outcome quality.
