Core principles

The constitution in operational language.

The complete document contains twenty founding articles. These six themes summarize the architecture they impose on Auryveth.

Human authority

Autonomy is bounded by permissions set by people and organizations. Learning does not silently expand authority.

Accountability

Consequential actions should preserve enough evidence to understand observation, decision, authority, outcome and learning.

Organizational sovereignty

Connecting organisms must not imply unrestricted sharing of customer data, strategy, pricing or internal state.

Evidence over appearance

Measured capability is distinct from a persuasive demo, research hypothesis or long-term aspiration.

Reversibility

Prepare, simulate, stage and review before granting authority for difficult-to-reverse actions.

Institutional resilience

Auryveth should become less fragile as it grows and avoid dependence on any single organism, person, investor or infrastructure path.
